Our Story

NEARLY 150 YEARS
IN THE MAKING.

1878

A CLUB IS BORN

Founded by a group of railway workers looking for a way to spend their Saturdays, the club started with little more than a shared kit and a patch of grass.

1910

HOME GROUND OPENS

The gates opened on the stadium that would become the heartbeat of the club, hosting generations of matchdays and a few famous nights that are still talked about today.

1968

EUROPEAN GLORY

Years of rebuilding after hardship culminated in the club's first major European trophy, a night that shaped the identity of every side that followed.

1999

THE TREBLE

A season that redefined what was possible, sealed by a stoppage-time finish that fans still describe as if it happened yesterday.

TODAY

A NEW CHAPTER

A new generation of players wears the shirt, carrying the same weight of history into every matchday, with fans old and new filling the stands.
